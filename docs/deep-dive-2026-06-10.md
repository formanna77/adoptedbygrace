## II. The Evidence of Invisibility

This is not speculation; the repo already contains the diagnosis, dated nineteen days ago (`gsc-findings-2026-05.md`, read from the verified GSC property):

- **3 clicks** in 3 months. 883 impressions. Average position **17.9** (page 2 ≈ zero traffic).
- **21 pages indexed; ~550 invisible to Google.** Flagships ("question-faithgift", "question-where-did-your-faith-come-from") **never crawled at all**.
- Top queries were peripheral (Luther biography, Münchhausen trilemma) — the soteriology core surfaces for nothing.
- Diagnosis in that doc, still correct: young domain + near-zero inbound editorial links → Google rations crawl and declines to index. On-page/technical SEO is **already excellent** (unique titles, unique meta descriptions, correct canonicals, clean sitemap, JSON-LD, open robots). The doorway is built; no road leads to it.

Fresh probes today (2026-06-10): web searches for `"adoptedbygrace.net"` and `"scripture tsunami" … adoptedbygrace` return nothing from the site. Nineteen days after the sitemap re-submit, visibility has not detectably moved. GotQuestions ranks for the exact questions our pages answer better.

And the sobering arithmetic: since the GSC diagnosis was written, the sessions have produced and perfected roughly 60 re-read pages and 8 net-new pages — all of the highest order, **97% of which Google will not show to anyone.** Every marginal hour on a new page currently yields approximately zero marginal readers. Every marginal hour on reach multiplies all 673 existing pages at once.

The site's own theology settles the priority. God ordains means as well as ends — and "how can they hear without someone preaching to them?" (Romans 10:14) is a question about roads, not about whether the sermon is finished. A rescue operation the drowning cannot find is a lifeboat in drydock.
