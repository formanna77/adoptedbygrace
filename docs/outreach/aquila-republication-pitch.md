**Subject:** Republication offer — two essays on the doctrines of grace

Dear editors,

I publish adoptedbygrace.net, a free, ad-free site making the Scripture-first case for the doctrines of grace. Since The Aquila Report draws much of its content from other publishers and bloggers, I'd like to offer two essays for republication — in full or excerpted, with attribution and a link back, no fee:

1. **"The Free Offer of the Gospel"** — https://adoptedbygrace.net/question-free-offer-of-the-gospel — why hyper-Calvinism and Arminianism make the same mistake about the gospel call: both assume the offer's sincerity requires ability in the sinner. Edwards's natural/moral distinction, Spurgeon's "Warrant of Faith," and the Canons of Dort's "promiscuously and without distinction" carry the argument. Written for church members who have heard both errors from adjacent pews.

2. **"Where Did Your Faith Come From?"** — https://adoptedbygrace.net/question-where-did-your-faith-come-from — a step-by-step walk from premises every believer already holds to the conclusion that faith itself is the gift, and that claiming credit for it is works-righteousness in disguise.

Both are original to the site and free for you to use. If republication interests you, I can send clean text in whatever format serves your workflow.
