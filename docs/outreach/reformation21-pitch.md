## The email (copy-paste; send from Aaron's identity)

Subject: Guest article pitch — "Where Did Your Faith Come From?"

Dear Dr. Stivason,

Cameron Shaffer's recent piece on church health opened with the question "Who can
change the sinner's heart?" I'd like to offer Reformation21 an original essay that
presses that question one step further back: where did the faith that trusted Christ
come from?

The essay walks a reader, step by step, from premises every evangelical already
holds to the conclusion that crediting our own faith is a quiet form of
works-righteousness — and that Ephesians 2:8-9, Philippians 1:29, and Acts 13:48
leave the believer nothing to boast in and everything to rest in. It's written in
the tradition of Spurgeon and Lewis: rigorous but readable, pastoral rather than
polemical, ending in assurance rather than argument.

I write at adoptedbygrace.net; a representative treatment is
https://adoptedbygrace.net/question-where-did-your-faith-come-from. I'm happy to
write the piece fresh for your readers at whatever length suits the site, and to
follow your editorial direction. A short author bio linking back to the site is all
I'd ask in return.

Would this be a fit?
