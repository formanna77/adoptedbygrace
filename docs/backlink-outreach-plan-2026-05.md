# Backlink Outreach Plan — adoptedbygrace.net

**Why this is the lever.** Google Search Console confirms the site is on page 2
(avg position 17.9) and has only ~21 of 599 pages indexed. Both problems trace
to the same root: a young domain with almost no inbound editorial links, so
Google neither trusts it enough to rank it nor spends crawl budget indexing it.
No on-page change fixes this. **Editorial backlinks from respected Reformed
sites** are what move a young domain — they are simultaneously a trust signal
(ranking) and a discovery path (crawling). This is slow, relationship-driven
work, but it is the highest-leverage activity available.

## Guardrails (white-hat only — non-negotiable)

- **Never buy links, join link exchanges, or use PBNs.** Google penalizes these,
  and they betray the site's integrity ethos. Every link must be *earned* because
  the content genuinely deserves it.
- **No spam.** Personalized, useful outreach only. One bad mass-blast can get the
  domain flagged.
- **Substack links are mostly nofollow** — great for reaching humans and earning
  *referral* traffic and restacks, but they pass little ranking authority. Keep
  cross-posting (per `SUBSTACK-CROSSPOSTING.md`), but don't count it as a
  backlink strategy.

## Tier 1 — Highest value, do first

**1. Monergism.com.** The single most valuable target in this niche. Monergism
is a curated directory of Reformed resources that the whole community uses, with
high domain authority. Getting flagship pages listed there is both a strong
backlink and a steady referral source. They accept resource submissions — submit
the crown-jewel and pillar pages (faith-as-gift, the doctrines-of-grace
explainer, the Romans 9 / John 6 pages, the Reformed Source Library). *(Pitch #1
below.)*

**2. The Aquila Report / Reformation21 / Place for Truth (Alliance of Confessing
Evangelicals).** Reformed publications that republish or link quality articles
and accept guest contributions. A guest piece with an author bio linking back is
a clean editorial link. *(Pitch #2 below.)*

**3. Reformed "resources" / "links" pages.** Many Reformed church and ministry
sites maintain a page of recommended external resources. Find them with searches
like `Reformed "recommended resources"`, `"doctrines of grace" resources links`,
`Calvinism "helpful links"`. Request inclusion with a specific, relevant page.
*(Pitch #3 below.)*

## Tier 2 — Community presence (referral + discovery, some links)

- **r/Reformed and r/Calvinism (Reddit).** Share genuinely helpful individual
  pages when they answer a live question. Links are nofollow but drive real
  readers, and exposure there often leads bloggers/pastors to link editorially.
  Be a contributor, not a self-promoter — answer first, link only when it truly
  helps.
- **The PuritanBoard and other Reformed forums.** Same principle: participate
  substantively; link a deep page when it directly answers a thread.
- **Christianity Stack Exchange / Quora.** Answer doctrines-of-grace questions
  thoroughly and cite the relevant page as a source. Nofollow, but high-intent
  referral and discovery.

## Tier 3 — Relationship-built, slower

- **Reformed bloggers and pastors.** Identify writers covering soteriology;
  email them when a specific page genuinely complements something they wrote.
  Offer it as a resource, not a favor.
- **Reformed podcasts.** Offer the author/owner as a guest on the faith-as-gift
  or total-depravity arguments; podcast show notes carry a backlink.
- **Seminary / campus ministry resource lists.** Reformed-leaning seminaries and
  campus groups sometimes list outside resources.

## Realistic cadence & expectations

- **Aim for 5–10 quality links over the next quarter**, not a flood. A handful of
  links from trusted Reformed sites will do more than dozens of low-quality ones.
- **Track outreach** in a simple list: target, date contacted, response, link
  acquired. Follow up once after ~2 weeks; never more.
- **Expect lag.** Backlinks lift a young domain over *months*, not days. Combined
  with the indexing fixes (sitemap re-submit, request-indexing) and the long-tail
  targeting already in place, this is the path off page 2.
