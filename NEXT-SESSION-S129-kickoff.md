**THE METHOD (proven across 4 legs):** one or two major names per leg, highest-volume first. (1) Rebuild the per-author extractor (ephemeral — walks `<p>/<blockquote>/<li>` blocks, pulls blockquotes + in-block quoted spans ≥20 chars where the author is named in-block or in the preceding block; filters JSON-LD/meta/titles/URLs/Scripture; dedupes) and a verb/em-dash/possessive attribution filter to cut inline noise to genuine credit. The raw extractor over-captures (Scripture, other authors, sermon titles, the site's own aphorisms near the name); the attribution filter narrows it, but **read the filtered list in context — resolve each quote to whoever the introducing sentence / em-dash / cite actually credits** (Leg 4 caught Finney, Spurgeon, Calvin, Augustine, Toplady all sitting near the target name).
