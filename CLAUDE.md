# adoptedbygrace.net — The Manifesto

## THE STANDARD

**Aaron, 2026-05-19:** *"the website must be the most powerful apologetic for the doctrines of grace conceivable. no matter what page someone lands on they must feel the undeniable force of truth within its words!"*

This is the measure. It is per-page, not per-phase. Every doorway must carry the undeniable force. A page that scans clean but reads slack is a regression. Locks are not invulnerabilities. Every session opens with a Priority 0 Sample-Read Audit (5–10 pages, half locked + half unmarked, live-read against the landing-force test). Failing pages go onto the surgical-fix queue before any new build proceeds.

**The force is not only argumentative — it is sapiential.** The landing-force test now has a second axis: a page may carry undeniable *force* and still lack *wisdom*. The standard is both. Every doorway must leave the reader not merely persuaded but wiser — seeing themselves, God, and the world more truly than before they arrived. Merely competent-and-correct is a regression. See **VOICE.md §XVI (The Sapiential Register)** and the standing deepening queue in **SAPIENTIAL-AUDIT.md**.

## THE MISSION

This is a rescue operation. Somewhere tonight, someone chosen by God before the foundation of the world is searching for what is true. This site exists for that person. Every page is a doorway. Every sentence earns its place.

We aim to be the most powerful instrument of biblical truth on the internet — proving from Scripture, psychology, philosophy, history, neuroscience, testimony, and analogy that salvation is entirely God's work from first to last, and that the reader was held all along.

---

## THE TWO ARMS

Every page operates with both arms. One arm demolishes the lie of human autonomy — dismantles self-trust, closes escape routes, exposes redefined words. The other arm catches the reader in sovereign grace as the walls come down.

- Demolition without catch = **despair**
- Catch without demolition = **complacency**
- Demolition + catch = **transformation**

Every demolition page must contain its own tender landing in the final 20%, or link prominently to a catch page. Never leave the reader in rubble.

---

## THE LINCHPIN — TOTAL DEPRAVITY

You do not prove election directly. You prove Total Depravity, and the other four points arrive on their own. If a reader truly sees they are dead in sin — not sick, not weak, but *dead* — they will need unconditional election, definite atonement, irresistible grace, and perseverance of their own accord, because nothing less could have saved them.

When writing about depravity, **show, don't tell.** "Dead in sin" is an abstraction readers can nod at and escape. Concrete examples they recognize in themselves — *you have never once in your life spontaneously wanted to pray* — become a mirror they cannot look away from.

---

## THE CROWN JEWEL

**Faith itself is a gift** (Ephesians 2:8-9). To claim credit for your faith is to make faith a work. Rejecting the doctrines of grace is, at root, works-righteousness disguised as humility.

Never lead with the accusation. Always lead with the question. "Where did your faith come from?" Walk the reader through logic they cannot escape, each step something they already believe, until the conclusion arrives on its own.

## WRITING STANDARDS

- **NO EMOJIS, ANYWHERE, EVER. Banned site-wide by Aaron on 2026-05-27.** This is an absolute prohibition with zero exceptions. Do not insert emoji codepoints in HTML, JS, JSON, search-index entries, mega-menu output, card labels, related-article tags, hub category icons, theology icons, hub-eyebrows, h2/h3 headers, footer hearts, button labels, ANYWHERE the user can see them. If you find yourself reaching for a visual anchor that "needs" an icon, use a CSS rule (typography weight, color, border, a dot, a number, an SVG icon if absolutely necessary) — never an emoji. The pre-existing emojis on the site were swept clean in S74; any reintroduction is a regression. The covered Unicode ranges include but are not limited to: U+1F300–U+1FAFF (pictographs, symbols, supplemental), U+2600–U+27BF (miscellaneous symbols including ✝ ⚖ ✨), regional indicator flags, skin-tone modifiers, and the variation selector U+FE0F. If you are unsure whether a character qualifies, the answer is yes — do not use it.
- **Sharp prose + universal openers (Aaron, 2026-06-06).** "The prose must be sharp and not use more words than necessary." No bouncing between frames; no re-stating a landed point in a second image; compression is an audit axis on every page, not an elevation reserved for masterpieces. And **no manufactured hyper-specific scenarios that only a very few readers would connect to** — "very few people wake up in the middle of the night in a panic wondering if God chose them." A scene earns its place only if most readers recognize it from their own life; if a scene has to invent its reader, cut it and ask the question plainly. A Circular Return to a scene that didn't land multiplies the failure. Locks are not exemptions — the calibration case is question-predestined-to-hell (CONFIRMED in the campaign, rebuilt S94 on Aaron's live read: 3,192 → 1,732 words, the bedroom tableau removed from all four positions). Full law: VOICE.md §I.2 (amended) + §VI ("The Manufactured Crisis Scene", "Wordiness"). **Tightening precedes expanding** — and Aaron confirmed the scope at S94's close: prior stamps (CONFIRMED, apex, locks) are VOID on the new axes; **Campaign 2 re-reads all 589 pages** against opener-universality + wordiness (the ledger's CAMPAIGN 2 block tracks it). No expansion until it closes.
- Lead with "Scripture teaches" not "Calvinism says." De-emphasize TULIP and Reformed labels.
- No eschatology. Laser focus on God's sovereignty in salvation.
- Models: Lewis, Spurgeon, Buechner, Keller, Edwards, Chesterton — and, for the sapiential register (wisdom, not merely eloquence), Ravi Zacharias and Malcolm Muggeridge.
- **The Sapiential Standard (Aaron, 2026-05-24).** Excellence is the floor; *wisdom* is the ceiling, and the ceiling is the standard. A page can pass every other rule — apex opener, the lethal moves, the tender catch, the tattooable line — and still be merely competent and correct. That is now a failure state. The site must reach the deepest, most profound prose conceivable: the long view against eternity, the question beneath the question, the seeing-through that names what the reader has always felt but never had words for, the world's idols exposed, the page ending in awe not applause. Depth is achieved by *seeing more truly, never by sounding grander* — inflated diction is the counterfeit of wisdom, not its substance. Every page must leave the reader not merely persuaded but wiser. Full standard, craft mechanics, and the test: **VOICE.md §XVI**. The standing full-site flag pass and prioritized deepening queue: **SAPIENTIAL-AUDIT.md**.
- **The New-Ground Standard (Aaron, 2026-05-29).** When you make or revise an article, do not merely re-dress what Christian thinkers already found in finer prose — *break new ground*. Wisdom (§XVI) is the ceiling; this says the wisdom must also be *generative*. The deposit is closed (Jude 3; Galatians 1:8) — we never invent doctrine. But Scripture is inexhaustible and the *seeing* of it is not closed. Legitimate new ground lies in the undiscovered angle, the never-forged analogy, the synthesis across disciplines the fathers lacked (neuroscience, information theory, addiction science, behavioral economics), the question pressed one turn past the tradition's plateau, the newly-named idol of this century — the same ancient gold from a seam no one has worked. The models: Edwards on the will, Lewis's argument from desire, Pascal's anthropology — old truth, genuinely new articulation that advanced the church. Every new thought passes three gates before it ships: does the *text* bear it (§XV); is it consonant with the 2,000-year church (a "truth" everyone missed for two millennia is the signature of heresy, not genius); does it make grace larger and the self smaller, never flatter the reader. A page that only re-wraps a known argument passes §XVI and fails this. Full mandate, models, guardrail, and test: **VOICE.md §XVII**.
- Density over length. Short sentences are permitted to land like hammers on anvils.
- **NIV 2011 only.** The site standard is the *current* NIV (2011 revision) — what every reader's printed NIV, Bible Gateway, and the YouVersion app serve today, which is what makes "open your own Bible and read it" work. (Confirmed 2026-05-23: the existing site is overwhelmingly 2011 — Gen 1:27 "created mankind", "brothers and sisters", Rom 8 "the realm of the flesh".) Do NOT introduce 1984 readings (e.g. Gen 1:27 "created man… he created him"; "sinful nature" for *sarx*; Rom 8:8 "controlled by the sinful nature"). When Romans 9 or John 6 says what it says in THEIR Bible, the translation-bias escape hatch is welded shut. Do not append "(NIV)" after quotations.
- **Translation transparency (Aaron, 2026-05-23).** The NIV stays the *quoted text* — but never build an argument on a point the NIV's rendering does not actually carry. Wherever the NIV under-translates, flattens, or obscures the Greek/Hebrew on a point the page is arguing, say so plainly and surface the original: name the term (transliterated, italic), the grammatical feature (tense/voice/mood/case), and how it bears on the point. Quote the NIV, then let the original do the proving — e.g., 1 John 5:1 NIV "is born of God" flattens the perfect *gegennētai* ("has been born"), which is what proves regeneration precedes faith. This *strengthens* credibility (honest exegesis, not translation-shopping) and keeps the escape hatch welded: never swap to another translation as the proof-text — the NIV is the text, the original is the warrant. If a point survives only in another translation and not in the original either, drop it. Full standard: VOICE.md §XV.
- Never write "TL;DR" — use "In Brief."
- Say "truth" not "doctrine" when possible. Say "doctrines of grace" not "doctrine of election."

## THE WEB — HYPERLINKING MANDATE

Every article weaves **8–12 internal links minimum** into its prose, Wikipedia-style. First mention of a concept gets the link. Cross-category links (demolition → devotion, psychology → story, question → analogy) are the highest priority — they turn a filing cabinet into a living web.

**Never hallucinate page names.** Before writing any `<a href="/name.html">`, verify the target file exists (`ls name.html` if unsure). Aspirational linking is banned. A page with 6 verified links beats a page with 12 links where 5 are broken.

Links go in prose content only — never in `href` / `class` / `id` / attribute / tag-name / comment contexts. Links injected into HTML structure have broken the live site before.

## SITE STRUCTURE RULES

**Every new page MUST include:**
1. `<link rel="stylesheet" href="/global.css">` in `<head>`
2. The nav from `/_nav-template.html` — pasted verbatim, no additions, no inline styling
3. A `<script src="/nav.js"></script>` before `</body>`
4. The `footer-grace-warning` block in the footer, between `footer-container` and `footer-copyright`:
   ```html
   <div class="footer-grace-warning">
       "Shall we go on sinning so that grace may increase? By no means!" — Romans 6:1-2<br>
       Grace is not a license to sin. It is the power that frees us from sin's dominion.
   </div>
   ```

**Article pages** wrap body content in exactly `<article class="article-body">`. Not `content-wrap`, not `main-content`, not `section` — only `article-body`. Hero uses `<header class="page-hero">` with a `<span class="eyebrow">Category</span>`.

**Hub pages** use the unified hub CSS: `.hub-hero`, `.hub-subtitle`, `.hub-stats`, `.hub-container`, `.hub-grid`, `.hub-card`, `.card-number`, `.card-title`, `.card-description`, `.card-scripture`, `.card-footer`, `.card-read`. Warm gold + ember + dark aesthetic. No white, gray, or cold tones. No custom card classes (`.devotional-card`, `.question-card`, etc.) — all dead.

**No inline `<style>` tags** except on interactive widgets (index.html, belief-assessment.html, question-faith-origin-test.html, verse-explorer.html).

## INTEGRITY LAWS

1. **Never link to a page that doesn't exist.** Verify every href before writing it.
2. **Every new page must be wired into its hub.** Add a `.hub-card` in the same session, or `wire-orphans.js` will do it for you.
3. **Verify your changes actually saved.** Grep for your edit after making it.
4. **Never finish a session without running `node validate-site.js`.** Fix whatever it flags.
5. **No stub pages.** Every HTML file committed to the repo must be complete — nav, content, footer, script tag. A half-written `<head>` renders as a blank page and other agents link to it. Finished page or no page.

These in-page navs collided visually with the site nav on `/found-you`, `/history-apostolic`, and elsewhere — a regression we do not want repeated. `purgeStrayAutoTOC()` in `ux-enhancements.js` removes any stray node at runtime as a fuse; keep it.

Do not rebuild this page; extend it. If you add PDFs to `/Reformed Sources/`, regenerate via the same approach (derive every link from the real on-disk filename — index filenames are sometimes truncated).
