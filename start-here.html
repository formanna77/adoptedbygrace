Your Journey Starts Here

Begin Your Path Into Grace

Most people come to these truths sideways — through a verse that would not sit still, or an objection they could not answer, or a life that stopped making sense on the other account. Below is a staircase of five phases — what these truths claim, how they hold together, where Scripture teaches them, what the objections weigh, and how to live held. Walk it in order, or step in where you stand. First-time reader? Start with how this site is built. Want the primary sources? Open the Source Library.

Most sites about salvation ask you to do something. This one asks you to notice what has already been done.

Every article on Adopted by Grace is built to show you, from whatever angle you arrive by — Scripture, philosophy, psychology, history, neuroscience, story — that the rescue you are hoping to accomplish has a name and a date older than the universe, and your only work is to see it. And if you find that you cannot make yourself want it — that is precisely the condition this whole gospel was written for.
