## THE FEELING-SLIP — FOUR forms, the DIRECTION LAW, the RECALL LAW, the GRAMMAR LAW

**THE DIRECTION LAW.** The slip is **grammatically identical** to a sanctioned move; only the OBJECT of the evidence-claim differs:

| interior state → evidence of… | verdict |
|---|---|
| **depravity / inability / resistance** | **SANCTIONED** — §II Move 4, the Corpse Inversion. Apex work. |
| **election / the Spirit / salvation** | **SLIP.** Fix it. |
| the claim stated *in order to be denied* | **MODEL.** Copy it. |

*"the strange way certain sentences on this site have stopped you mid-scroll"* (S189, counter-cs-lewis — the reader's own reading offered as proof of drawing) and *"if the resistance has loosened even a little — He is already prying"* are the same shape and opposite theology. **No phrase-grep can separate them.** `detect-feeling-slip.js` v3 classifies by object; its test ORDER (**REPUDIATE/DENIED → STRONG_ELECT → DEPRAVE → ELECT+GOD_REF**) is load-bearing and must not be reordered.

**THE RECALL LAW (S183).** The classifier only sees what its trigger vocabulary was taught to name. **If you extend it, add to `INTERIOR`/`BARE`/`PREDICATE` — and never add a token that also appears in `ELECT`/`STRONG_ELECT`, or the sentence certifies itself.** `drawn` and `moved` are excluded for exactly this reason. **THE PRICE OF THAT GUARD IS A PERMANENT HAND-READ OBLIGATION.** S186 found one; S187 two; S188 seven; **S189 found three more in a six-page sample-read, every one on a page the detector called clean.** These are found only by hand cold-read, never by the tool. **The P0 Sample-Read Audit has now paid for itself FOUR consecutive sessions. Never skip it.**

**THE GRAMMAR LAW (S185).** Vocabulary is only half of recall. v3 has three shapes: *the fact that you…*, the determiner (*that/this/your + INTERIOR*), and the **PREDICATE** (*you are/you feel/you find yourself + PREDICATE*). A fourth shape almost certainly exists and has never been looked for.

1. **Classic:** a FEELING / comprehension / the-act-of-still-reading read as EVIDENCE the reader IS saved. **Reusable models:** S185's *"Any instrument that reads chosen when you feel warm and lost when you feel cold is measuring the weather. Put it down."*; S186's *"A summons is not verified by the quality of the hearing it produced, and Lazarus was not asked afterward to describe the voice."*; S188's *"the author never asked you to read the decree off your own chest"*; **S189's** — counter-cs-lewis's *"do not turn your persistence into a verdict about yourself — the pursued rarely hears the Hound until He is near; the pursuit is His, and it does not fail for want of your noticing"*; romans-9's *"Nothing here depends on how the reading felt. 2. **INVERSE:** absence of a prescribed feeling read as unbelief, or prescribed as the *test of understanding*. **Never tell a reader what he felt** (S189: dialogue-arminian pinned eternal security to *"you finally felt the hand on your back"* → recast to *"if you are His"*; never-gives-up's *"a small flinch… the breath you just held a half-second too long"* → softened to *"if something in you tightens against it"*).
3. **Slips hide in meta / pullquotes / In-Briefs / FAQ-JSON-LD / closing poems / quiz answer keys / BAKED HUB CARDS / WIDGET DATA ARRAYS — and in the CLOSING CATCH SECTION, the densest slip real-estate on the site.** S189's dialogue slip was the last line of a sermon-within-the-dialogue.
