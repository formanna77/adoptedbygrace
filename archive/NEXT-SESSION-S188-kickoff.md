## THE FEELING-SLIP — FOUR forms, the DIRECTION LAW, the RECALL LAW, the GRAMMAR LAW

**THE DIRECTION LAW.** The slip is **grammatically identical** to a sanctioned move; only the OBJECT of the evidence-claim differs:

| interior state → evidence of… | verdict |
|---|---|
| **depravity / inability / resistance** | **SANCTIONED** — §II Move 4, the Corpse Inversion. Apex work. |
| **election / the Spirit / salvation** | **SLIP.** Fix it. |
| the claim stated *in order to be denied* | **MODEL.** Copy it. |

*"something in you is leaning toward Him — that leaning is the drawing"* and *"something in you is rising to defend the story you tell about yourself"* are the same sentence shape and opposite theology. **No phrase-grep can separate them.** `detect-feeling-slip.js` v3 classifies by object; its test ORDER (**REPUDIATE/DENIED → STRONG_ELECT → DEPRAVE → ELECT+GOD_REF**) is load-bearing and must not be reordered.

**THE RECALL LAW (S183).** The classifier only sees what its trigger vocabulary was taught to name. **If you extend it, add to `INTERIOR`/`BARE`/`PREDICATE` — and never add a token that also appears in `ELECT`/`STRONG_ELECT`, or the sentence certifies itself.** `drawn` and `moved` are excluded for exactly this reason. **THE PRICE OF THAT GUARD IS A PERMANENT HAND-READ OBLIGATION** — S186 found `apologetic-tetagmenoi-acts-13-48`'s live slip on the excluded word `drawn`; **S187 found TWO the detector cannot see** — `the-mirror` reveal-12 (*"That hesitation is the Spirit"*, inside a JS data array) and `demolition-prevenient-grace` (*"if it felt like waking… you have already experienced what this article is describing"* — conversion phenomenology read as proof of monergism). **These are found only by hand cold-read, never by the tool.**

**THE GRAMMAR LAW (S185).** Vocabulary is only half of recall. v3 has three shapes: *the fact that you…*, the determiner (*that/this/your + INTERIOR*), and the **PREDICATE** (*you are/you feel/you find yourself + PREDICATE*). A fourth shape almost certainly exists and has never been looked for.

1. **Classic:** a FEELING / comprehension / the-act-of-still-reading read as EVIDENCE the reader IS saved. **Reusable models:** S185's *"Any instrument that reads chosen when you feel warm and lost when you feel cold is not measuring the decree. It is measuring the weather. Put it down."*; S186's *"A summons is not verified by the quality of the hearing it produced, and Lazarus was not asked afterward to describe the voice."*; **S187's** `analogy-doctor-corpse` fix — *"do not turn that stirring into a mirror and start grading your own reflection for signs of life… Your assurance was never built to rest on whether your chest is warm right now. It rests on a Doctor who does not lose the ones He raises"* — and `the-mirror` reveal-12 — *"If you are His, it was never your grip that closed."*
2. **INVERSE:** absence of a prescribed feeling read as unbelief (or, S187: prescribed as the *test of understanding* — the `systematic-election` "charge in the hair on your arms" line).
3. **Slips hide in meta / pullquotes / In-Briefs / FAQ-JSON-LD / closing poems / quiz answer keys / BAKED HUB CARDS — and in WIDGET DATA ARRAYS** (S185 and S187: the-mirror's worst instances sat inside JS object literals). **The CLOSING CATCH SECTION is the densest slip real-estate on the site.**
