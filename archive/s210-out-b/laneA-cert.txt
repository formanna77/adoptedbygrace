Which is why the freedman's last temptation is the strangest one.</p>

   <p>Out on the road, a mile clear of the cell, he is still working his wrists — still testing whether the ache means the irons were truly struck. It is the old bondage in a fresh uniform. No freed man ever kept his freedom in his feelings; he kept it in the word of the one who signed the paper. Some walk out of that cell weeping and some walk out numb, wondering why they cannot feel what they are told they ought to feel; <a href="/devotional-my-chains-fell-away">no chain ever fell off because a prisoner finally wanted it off.</a> The gate does not swing shut behind the second man.</p>

   <p>The Son holds the key, the authority, and the blood-bought right to use it. He did not open that door to stand outside it and watch whether you walk out convincingly enough to have deserved the opening. He opened it, and He walked you out Himself.</p>

ANCHOR:
And here is the mercy at the center: if, even now, the gospel is beginning to look less foolish than it did an hour ago — if something you have read a hundred times is faintly, unaccountably starting to shine — that is not the achievement of your mind finally catching up. The darkened mind does not light itself, any more than the void on the first day decided to glow. If you are seeing anything at all, it is because God has begun to speak. That faint dawn in you is not your discovery. The ache is the dawn.
END_ANCHOR
REPLACEMENT:
And here is the mercy at the center — take it carefully, because there is a way of receiving it that quietly gives back everything the argument just removed. If something you have read a hundred times is faintly starting to shine, the reflex is to turn and appraise the shine: is it real, is it enough, is it more than it was last month. That is the darkened mind being handed the one assignment the whole argument above has just proved it cannot carry. You would be measuring the dawn with the eyes the dawn came to open, and filing your own report as the verdict.</p>

   <p>Nobody has ever been asked to vouch for a sunrise. It comes up on an authority entirely outside the people it wakes, and it does not take the roll first. So if you feel nothing this morning — if none of this has moved you and you are tired of being told that it should — you have not been left outside of it. <a href="/systematic-regeneration">What is given to the blind is given while they are still blind.</a>
