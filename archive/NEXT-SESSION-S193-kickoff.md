- `node detect-gospel-absence.js <page>.html` — now **prints the span it measured** above the verdict. Read it. Never trust the verdict without it.

**TRIAGE, NOT VERDICT, and S192 re-proved it: the large majority land on a Person by pronoun — "He," the Shepherd, the Gardener, "Someone" — and are NOT defective.** The Trinity is not a scoring rubric. Ask the real question: **does the reader end with a Person, or with a proposition?** The defect classes, in descending value:

1. **The page ends in furniture with no catch at all** — the last prose is a nav paragraph or a card deck. S192's third pass now catches most trailing decks automatically; what remains are the **small** ones (fewer than 3 links, or a single card), which no measurement will separate from prose. Read for them.
2. **The catch is a sitemap paragraph** — three to five links wearing a closing voice. Fix by **moving the nav block off the end** (ninety seconds, and the page gets its hammer back: `question-unconditional` S191, `just-realized` and `response-roger-olson` S192).
3. **The catch outsources its own landing** — `question-what-must-i-do-to-be-saved` ended by handing the reader to Spurgeon's PDF. A page that admits it does not land, does not land.
4. **The catch lands on a proposition** — beautiful, true, Person-less (`philosophy-gravity-of-grace`: *"It is rest."*). Easiest to fix well, because **the page's own image almost always runs straight to Christ if you look**: gravity's un-shieldability ran to *"Cursed is everyone who is hung on a pole"*; the jailer's keys ran to *"I hold the keys of death and Hades."*
5. **The catch is a feeling-slip** — see §C; this class overlaps §A heavily, because a page that lands on the reader's interior is a page that did not land on Christ.

**The craft law is unchanged and absolute: the catch is DERIVED FROM THE PAGE'S OWN CENTRAL IMAGE, never bolted on.** And one practical note from S192: on allegory pages (`story-thought-*`, `analogy-*`) **name Him plainly at least once in the catch.** Four S192 closes landed unmistakably on the risen Christ *described* and the detector still read NO LANDING — which was the right answer for a searcher landing cold, not a detector artifact.
