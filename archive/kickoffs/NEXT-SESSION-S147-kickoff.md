## §XIX — THE PREVENIENT-GRACE SWEEP (highest-value, corpus-wide; carry it forward)
The site repeatedly treats **John 6:44 / "draws" / "no one can come" / "choose [life]" / a universal-offer text** as a checkmate against free will. The *able* Wesleyan Arminian grants every one of those and answers with **prevenient grace** (the Father draws everyone — John 12:32 — enabling a freed-but-*resistible* response). Wherever a page leans on such a text, it MUST name prevenient grace and answer it at full strength — and **each page needs its OWN angle** (stamping the same answer everywhere is the §XVIII failure). Angles already spent in S146 (do NOT reuse — derive a fresh one from §XX):
- **ballot-box / "last room beneath the floor"** (question-cannot) — a universal enabling just relocates the decisive vote back to the autonomous will.
- **will-follows-its-loves** (question-freewill) — a neutral restored ability still chooses what the will loves; only a new heart changes the loves.
- **Pentecost differential** (demolition-acts2-21) — if grace is universal-resistible, the will is what saved the 3,000, overturning Rom 3:11.
- **John 6 self-definition** (demolition-john3-16) — drawn→come→raised is one unbroken motion (6:37/39); John 12:32 "all people" = all nations, not all individuals.
- **1 John 5:1 perfect *gegennētai*** (question-faithgift) — the new birth precedes the believing; faith is its cry. (Also: concede *touto* in Eph 2:8 is grammatically contestable; rest the weight on Phil 1:29.)
- **sourcehood / aseity** (systematic-compatibilism) — the libertarian's self-origination chased to its root asks the creature to be *causa sui*, which is God's alone.
- **typology-mechanism** (question-ezekiel37) — grant the corporate/national referent, then defend that the *mechanism* (God speaks life into the unable dead) is what the NT reuses (Eph 2:1,5).

Fresh angles still on the bench: the genetic-fallacy of a "neutral" will; the regress of the deciding factor; Acts 13:48 *tetagmenoi*; Lydia/Acts 16:14 *diēnoixen*; the asymmetry that prevenient grace must be EITHER too weak (saves no one) OR regeneration by a smaller name.
