=== PAGE: author
VERDICT: CONFIRMED-CLEAN
OPENER: Inversion — "You came looking for the author. Let me save you the trouble: there is not much to find, and what little there is, I did not make." "I am not an authority on grace. I am a specimen of it" is the page in one line.
CLOSE: lands once on the passive elattousthai (even his decreasing is done to him), then stacks: "do not linger on the doorman. / Walk in. / The wedding has already begun... / He must become greater. / Let Him." Five terminal beats, and the last word is an imperative where the standard wants a statement. It survives because the imperative is a release, not a burden — the page has just denied the reader can do it — and because the wedding image is John 3:29, not an imported frame. Note for the ledger: "Walk in." is already a spent closing cadence corpus-wide.
CLAUSE: A specimen of grace, not an authority on it
=== END PAGE
