## STANDING FINDINGS (carry; do not re-derive)

- **A DETECTOR THAT MEASURES FORM CANNOT SEE AN ABSENT DESTINATION (S190).** Before building the next detector, ask what it measures — and whether anything measures whether the page *arrives*. The whole class of "the page is well-formed and goes nowhere" was invisible until something looked for it directly.
- **A PROMISE THE PAGE MAKES IS A DEBT (S189).** When a page says "hold that question," grep the rest of the page for the payoff before calling it done. A recap that makes the page *feel* finished is the alibi.
- **MEASURE THE SAME THING BEFORE AND AFTER THE PIPELINE (S188).** A queue that refills itself looks exactly like a queue that is merely large.
- **A GUARD AND ITS DETECTOR SHARE A MODEL, SO THEY SHARE ITS BLIND SPOTS — BUT NOT THEIR ERROR BUDGET (S188).** Containment beats vocabulary. Do NOT re-apply the entity-decode fix to the detectors; it was tried and reverted.
- **A REMEMBERED LIST HAS ALREADY DRIFTED — MEASURE, DON'T GUESS FROM NAMES (S188).**
- **A RULE WITH NOTHING CHECKING IT HAS ALREADY ROTTED (S186).** S190's proof: `.cta-section`/`.cta-quote`/`.cta-attribution` were used on the homepage and had **no rules anywhere in `global.css`** — the front door's closing section had been rendering unstyled, and nothing anywhere would ever have said so.
- **WHEN A HEURISTIC IS NOISY, LOOK FOR GROUND TRUTH ALREADY IN THE REPO (S186/S188).** **Verify EVERY quote against `scripture-niv.js` BEFORE you write it** — S190 verified all 26 additions pre-write and none is flagged divergent.
- **A RAW COUNT RANKS BY PAGE LENGTH; A DEFECT RANKS BY DENSITY (S184).**
- **A DETECTOR'S FALSE-POSITIVE CLASSES ARE PART OF ITS SPEC (S185/S188).** The apex Circular Return will always trip `detect-self-repeating-close.js` and is not a defect. `verify-scripture`'s DIVERGENT count is inflated by `all-content.html` card text — check whether a flagged page's *own* quotes are implicated before touching anything.
- **AN INTERACTIVE PAGE CAN LIE TO ITS READER IN CODE, NOT PROSE (S185/S187).** `node --check` extracted inline JS and re-parse JSON-LD after any edit.
