## THE FEELING-SLIP — FOUR forms, the DIRECTION LAW, the RECALL LAW, and now the GRAMMAR LAW

**THE DIRECTION LAW.** The slip is **grammatically identical** to a sanctioned move; only the OBJECT of the evidence-claim differs:

| interior state → evidence of… | verdict |
|---|---|
| **depravity / inability / resistance** | **SANCTIONED** — §II Move 4, the Corpse Inversion. Apex work. |
| **election / the Spirit / salvation** | **SLIP.** Fix it. |
| the claim stated *in order to be denied* | **MODEL.** Copy it. |

*"Something in you is rising to defend the story you tell about yourself"* and *"something in you is leaning toward Him — that leaning is the drawing"* are the same sentence shape and opposite theology. **No phrase-grep can separate them.** `detect-feeling-slip.js` v3 classifies by object; its test ORDER (**REPUDIATE/DENIED → STRONG_ELECT → DEPRAVE → ELECT+GOD_REF**) is load-bearing and must not be reordered.

**THE RECALL LAW (S183).** The classifier only sees what its trigger vocabulary was taught to name. **If you extend it, add to `INTERIOR`/`BARE`/`PREDICATE` — and never add a token that also appears in `ELECT`/`STRONG_ELECT`, or the sentence certifies itself.** (`drawn` and `moved` are excluded for exactly this reason. Do not add them.)

**THE GRAMMAR LAW (S185, new).** Vocabulary is only half of recall. Ask what SHAPES the trigger can express. v3 now has three: *the fact that you…*, the determiner form (*that/this/your + INTERIOR*), and the **PREDICATE** form (*you are/you feel/you find yourself + PREDICATE*). A fourth shape almost certainly exists and has never been looked for.

1. **Classic:** a FEELING / comprehension / the-act-of-still-reading read as EVIDENCE the reader IS saved. **Reusable models:** every-world's in-prose repudiation; phase2's *"it can be read from outside your own weather"*; S180's *"the light does not depend on the quality of your looking"*; S182's *"No one has ever read his own name off the inside of his own chest"*; S183's *"Fear certifies nothing"* · *"a needle has no access to the field that turned it"*; **S185's** *"Any instrument that reads chosen when you feel warm and lost when you feel cold is not measuring the decree. Put it down."* · *"a mirror that starts certifying the face in it has stopped being a mirror"* · *"an argument can unsettle a man without saving him, and a man can be saved and sit through this entirely unmoved."*
2. **INVERSE:** absence of a prescribed feeling read as unbelief.
3. **Slips hide in meta / pullquotes / In-Briefs / FAQ-JSON-LD / closing poems / quiz answer keys / BAKED HUB CARDS — and in WIDGET DATA ARRAYS** (S185: the worst instance on the site sat inside a JS object literal in `the-objection-collapse`). **And the CLOSING CATCH SECTION is the densest slip real-estate on the site.**
