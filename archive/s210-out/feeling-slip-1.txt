Go back to the day in your testimony, the one you have always told as the day you chose Him. Keep it; it is precious; it really happened. Only now tell it true. Underneath your reaching was His. Before your prayer was His call. Before the branch ever turned toward the light, a hand it could not see had planted it in the living Vine.</p>

   <p>And if you go looking for that day and come back with nothing — no scene worth the name, no reaching you would put any weight on, only a blur where other people keep a story — nothing underneath you has moved. A grafted branch does not remember the knife. Nothing was asked of it while the cut was made, and nothing is asked of it afterward to certify the join; the vine settles that, by keeping it alive. So the question was never whether you can find your own hand in the story. It is whose hand held the knife. The sap that came up into a dead branch never stopped to ask the branch how it felt about the arrangement — it simply came, and it comes still, and it will not stop until there is fruit on that branch that outlasts the branch.</p>
END_REPLACEMENT
