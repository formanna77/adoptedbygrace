ANCHOR:
   <p>This is the experience of everyone who has lived both sides of this revolution. They build intellectual towers to defend God—towers that require them to be the center, the one who can reason his way to faith, who can choose and decide and control. And God shatters them. He brings a person to the place where there is nothing left but surrender—where the only choice is to accept that there was no choice, that they had been chosen long before their choosing could even be possible.</p>
   
   <p>And in that surrender, he found not diminishment but glory. Not powerlessness but peace. Not insignificance but the unspeakable privilege of being held by infinite love.</p>
   
   <p>That is the Copernican Revolution of the soul. Not dethronement. Deliverance.</p>

   <div class="section-number">06</div>
   <h2 class="section-title">The Sun Still Rises in the East</h2>
   <div class="section-body">
   <p>Tomorrow morning — if God grants you tomorrow — the sun will rise in the east and set in the west. It will look exactly the way it has always looked. The sensation of standing on firm ground will be indistinguishable from the sensation you had before you knew the Earth moved. Nothing will <em>feel</em> different.</p>

   <p>But everything will <em>be</em> different. Because you will know that what feels like standing still is actually hurtling through space at sixty-seven thousand miles per hour, held in orbit by a gravitational force so vast and so gentle that you cannot feel it working. You will know that the sunrise is not the sun moving toward you but <em>you</em> being turned toward the sun — slowly, inevitably, by a force that was set in motion long before you existed.</p>

   <p>That is <a href="/question-how-grace-feels">what grace feels like</a> from the inside. It feels like your own decision. It feels like you reached for God. It feels like you are standing still and the gospel is moving toward you. But the truth — the Copernican truth, the truth that rewrites the model without changing the experience — is that you were being turned. Drawn. Orbited into the light by a gravity you did not create and cannot resist. And the vertigo you feel right now, reading this, is not the ground giving way. It is the ground <em>finally becoming real</em> beneath you — because for the first time, you are standing on something that does not depend on you to hold it up.</p>

   <p>The sun rises. You orbit. And the God at the center is not diminished by your smallness. He is glorified by it. Because what He holds, He holds forever.</p>

REPLACEMENT:
   <p>Everyone who has lived on both sides of this revolution tells it the same way. You build towers to defend God, and every tower needs you standing at the top of it: reasoning upward, deciding, keeping the whole structure plumb. Then God takes the towers down. You lose nothing you actually had. You were never on the throne, only tired from holding the pose — and you had been chosen long before you could choose. From the ground, you can see the whole sky.</p>

   <div class="section-number">06</div>
   <h2 class="section-title">The Sun Still Rises in the East</h2>
   <div class="section-body">
   <p>Tomorrow morning — if God grants you tomorrow — the sun will rise in the east and set in the west. It will look exactly the way it has always looked. The ground under your feet will feel exactly as still as it felt before you knew the Earth moved. Nothing will <em>feel</em> different.</p>

   <p>But everything will <em>be</em> different. You will know that what feels like standing still is hurtling through space at sixty-seven thousand miles per hour, held in orbit by a force so vast and so gentle that you cannot feel it working. You will know that sunrise is not the sun coming toward you but <em>you</em> being turned toward the sun — slowly, certainly, by something set in motion long before you existed.</p>

   <p>That is <a href="/question-how-grace-feels">what grace feels like</a> from the inside. It feels like your own decision. It feels like you reached for God, like you were the one who moved. The experience is not a lie. The model was. You were being turned. Drawn into the light by a gravity you did not generate and could not have resisted.</p>

   <p>This is why it can feel, at first, like the ground moving. The ground is not giving way. It is becoming real — for the first time you are standing on something that does not depend on you to hold it up. The sun rises. You orbit. And the God at the center is not diminished by your smallness; He is glorified by it. Let the sun come up tomorrow and look exactly the way it always has. You were never the one turning it.</p>
