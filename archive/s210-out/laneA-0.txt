VERDICT: NEEDS-WORK

CLOSE: feeling-certificate

=== PAGE: apologetic-chosen-as-firstfruits
VERDICT: CONFIRMED-CLEAN
OPENER: Inversion by concession — grants the objector the surface reading ("doesn't that mean God's choice ran through your believing?"), then reverses it on the sentence's architecture: one destination, two roads. Meets the strongest opponent first.
CLOSE: lands once / clean — the two roads of 2 Thess 2:13 converge on John 14:6, where Christ names Himself both of them. That convergence is the best single argumentative move in the batch, and everything after it descends from it to "You were chosen for Him." Assurance is grounded in believing, an act, never in a felt temperature.
CLAUSE: Paul's two roads meet a Person who calls Himself both

=== PAGE: apologetic-darkened-in-their-understanding
VERDICT: NEEDS-WORK
OPENER: Universal Ache stated as a puzzle everyone has watched — "people of staggering intelligence look at the gospel and see nothing in it" — then resolved by reading Paul's two "because" clauses backward. "The mind goes dark last, not first" is genuine new ground. Opener is excellent.
CLOSE: feeling-certificate, then multiplied and self-contradicting
CLAUSE: the mind goes dark last, the shadow of a hardened heart
NOTE: "The dead do not perceive the light, and the contentedly blind do not ache toward it. The ache is the dawn" is the most nearly verbatim instance of the banned certificate in this batch — and the page then contradicts its own certificate two sentences later ("Whatever you can or cannot see this morning, the light does not depend on the quality of your looking"), so it both demands the temperature and withdraws the demand, landing four separate times on the way out.
=== END PAGE
=== PAGE: apologetic-ekloge-the-greek-of-election
VERDICT: NEEDS-WORK
OPENER: Anaphoric catalog of escape routes — "He can flee to the foreknowledge clause and rebrand divine election as divine forecast" — four doors named and closed in sequence. Not one of the four named shapes, but it carries force and earns the page's premise. Opener is not the reason for the verdict.
CLOSE: no Savior / multiplied
CLAUSE: every escape route runs into the Greek and stops
NOTE: Across the entire close of a 4,042-word flagship there is no Jesus, no cross, no Son — the catch lands on grammar and an attribute instead of a Person, hammering "the Greek" three times in the last five sentences ("The Greek does not work the other way. The offered prayer partly redeems the earlier "The bending is the Spirit's first work," but the page still exits on a language fact.

=== PAGE: apologetic-he-did-not-spare-his-own-son
VERDICT: CONFIRMED-CLEAN
OPENER: Inversion — takes the verse everyone already loves and asks what it requires to work ("The argument only holds if the giving-up of the Son actually secured the all things for the same people it was for"), so the comfort itself becomes the proof of definite atonement.
CLOSE: lands once / clean — "He will. He cannot not." and "The hand that will not let you go is nail-marked" reach the Person directly. Notably it refuses the certificate the rest of this batch keeps issuing: the trembling believer is not told the trembling proves anything, only that the question is "did He spare His Son?"
CLAUSE: the comfort of Romans 8:32 requires a cross that secured
