FILE: demolition-isaiah5-1-7.html
  REF : Matthew 21:43
  PAGE: a people who will produce its fruit.
  NIV : A landowner planted a vineyard with walls, a winepress, and watchtower, then leased it to farmers. At harvest time, he sent servants to collect his share. The tenants beat one servant, killed another, and stoned a third. When the owner sent additional servants, they received the same treatment. Finally, the owner sent his son, thinking they would respect him. Instead, the tenants said, "This is the heir. Let's kill him and take his inheritance." They threw him out of the vineyard and killed him. Jesus asked his listeners what the owner would do to these tenants. They replied he would destroy them and lease the vineyard to others who would give him his rightful share. Jesus then referenced Scripture: "The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone; the Lord has done this, and it is marvelous in our eyes." He declared that the kingdom of God would be taken from them and given to people producing its fruit. Whoever falls on this stone will be broken; whoever it falls upon will be crushed. When the chief priests and Pharisees heard these parables, they recognized Jesus was speaking about them. They sought to arrest him but feared the crowd, who believed him to be a prophet.
