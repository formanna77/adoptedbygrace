## THE FEELING-SLIP — FOUR forms, the DIRECTION LAW, and the S183 RECALL LAW

**THE DIRECTION LAW (why this family survived eleven sessions of greps).** The slip is **grammatically identical** to a sanctioned move; only the OBJECT of the evidence-claim differs:

| interior state → evidence of… | verdict |
|---|---|
| **depravity / inability / resistance** | **SANCTIONED** — §II Move 4, the Corpse Inversion. Apex work. |
| **election / the Spirit / salvation** | **SLIP.** Fix it. |
| the claim stated *in order to be denied* | **MODEL.** Copy it. |

*"Something in you is rising to defend the story you tell about yourself"* and *"something in you is leaning toward Him — that leaning is the drawing"* are the same sentence shape and opposite theology. **No phrase-grep can separate them.** `detect-feeling-slip.js` v2 classifies by object; its test ORDER (**REPUDIATE/DENIED → STRONG_ELECT → DEPRAVE → ELECT+GOD_REF**) is load-bearing and must not be reordered. STRONG_ELECT was inserted at S183 because a sentence merely *mentioning* resistance while claiming election was mis-filed SANCTIONED.

**THE RECALL LAW (S183).** The classifier only sees what its trigger vocabulary was taught to name. v1 knew *stirring/ache/leaning* and missed *flicker, flinch, instinct, impulse, softening, warmth, tug*; it required "the fact that YOU" adjacently; it had no `your <gerund>` form and no adjective slot. **If you extend it, add to `INTERIOR`/`BARE` — and never add a token that also appears in `ELECT`, or the sentence certifies itself** (the `if the drawing…` bug, caught and documented in the header).

## ELEVEN STANDING FINDINGS

- **A RESIDUE CHECK THAT GREPS THE PHRASE DOES NOT FIND THE SHAPE.** S181's lesson. **When you break a mold, grep its SHAPE and run the detectors, not just the phrase you fixed.**
- **A SHAPE-GREP DOES NOT FIND THE DIRECTION.** S182's lesson. Where a defect and a sanctioned move share a grammar, only semantic classification separates them.
- **AND NOW: A DIRECTION-CLASSIFIER DOES NOT FIND WHAT ITS VOCABULARY NEVER LEARNED TO NAME.** S183's lesson. Precision is cheap to verify and recall is not; **the only way to measure recall is to hand-read a stratum the detector claims is clean.** Budget for it every session.
- **A DETECTOR THAT REPORTS THE FIX AS THE DEFECT IS MIS-BUILT.** S183: `DENIED` lacked possessives and appositives, so it flagged S182's own corrective lines (*"is not YOUR proof"*, *"not a proof you were handed"*). **After extending any classifier, re-run it over the previous session's fixes and confirm they read MODEL.**
