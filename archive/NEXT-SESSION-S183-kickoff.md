## THE FEELING-SLIP — FOUR forms, and the S182 DIRECTION LAW

**THE DIRECTION LAW (the reason this family survived eleven sessions of greps).** The slip is **grammatically identical** to a sanctioned move; only the OBJECT of the evidence-claim differs:

| interior state → evidence of… | verdict |
|---|---|
| **depravity / inability / resistance** | **SANCTIONED** — §II Move 4, the Corpse Inversion. Apex work. |
| **election / the Spirit / salvation** | **SLIP.** Fix it. |
| the claim stated *in order to be denied* | **MODEL.** Copy it. |

*"Something in you is rising to defend the story you tell about yourself"* and *"something in you is leaning toward Him — that leaning is the drawing"* are the same sentence shape and opposite theology. **No phrase-grep can separate them.** `detect-feeling-slip.js` classifies by object; its test ORDER (REPUDIATE/DENIED → DEPRAVE → ELECT) is load-bearing and must not be reordered.

1. **Classic:** a FEELING / comprehension / the-act-of-still-reading read as EVIDENCE the reader IS saved. **Reusable models:** every-world's in-prose repudiation; phase2's *"it can be read from outside your own weather"*; S180's *"the light does not depend on the quality of your looking"* · *"stop auditing the turn"*; S181's devotional-i-cant-feel-god (*"the holding was never conditional on your feeling it"*); and **S182's** *"No one has ever read his own name off the inside of his own chest"* (question-am-i-chosen) · *"the effectual call issues no receipt"* (systematic-effectual-calling) · *"do not go listening at your own ribs for wind"* (systematic-regeneration).
2. **INVERSE:** absence of a prescribed feeling read as unbelief. shattered-lens-bible-doesnt-make-sense carried form 1 and form 2 in **consecutive sentences** (S182).
3. **Slips hide in meta / pullquotes / In-Briefs / FAQ-JSON-LD / closing poems / quiz answer keys.** **S182 proved this is where the WORST ones live** — the two most damaging instances on the site were FAQPage JSON-LD answers, i.e. rich-snippet text, on the two pages a frightened reader is likeliest to land on.
