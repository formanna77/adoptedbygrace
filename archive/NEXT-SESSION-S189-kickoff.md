## THE FEELING-SLIP — FOUR forms, the DIRECTION LAW, the RECALL LAW, the GRAMMAR LAW

**THE DIRECTION LAW.** The slip is **grammatically identical** to a sanctioned move; only the OBJECT of the evidence-claim differs:

| interior state → evidence of… | verdict |
|---|---|
| **depravity / inability / resistance** | **SANCTIONED** — §II Move 4, the Corpse Inversion. Apex work. |
| **election / the Spirit / salvation** | **SLIP.** Fix it. |
| the claim stated *in order to be denied* | **MODEL.** Copy it. |

*"something in you is leaning toward Him — that leaning is the drawing"* and *"something in you is rising to defend the story you tell about yourself"* are the same sentence shape and opposite theology. **No phrase-grep can separate them.** `detect-feeling-slip.js` v3 classifies by object; its test ORDER (**REPUDIATE/DENIED → STRONG_ELECT → DEPRAVE → ELECT+GOD_REF**) is load-bearing and must not be reordered.

**THE RECALL LAW (S183).** The classifier only sees what its trigger vocabulary was taught to name. **If you extend it, add to `INTERIOR`/`BARE`/`PREDICATE` — and never add a token that also appears in `ELECT`/`STRONG_ELECT`, or the sentence certifies itself.** `drawn` and `moved` are excluded for exactly this reason. **THE PRICE OF THAT GUARD IS A PERMANENT HAND-READ OBLIGATION.** S186 found one on the excluded word `drawn`; S187 found two the detector cannot see; **S188 found SEVEN in a single 8-page sample-read, every one on a page the detector called clean.** These are found only by hand cold-read, never by the tool. **The P0 Sample-Read Audit has now paid for itself three consecutive sessions. Never skip it.**

**THE GRAMMAR LAW (S185).** Vocabulary is only half of recall. v3 has three shapes: *the fact that you…*, the determiner (*that/this/your + INTERIOR*), and the **PREDICATE** (*you are/you feel/you find yourself + PREDICATE*). A fourth shape almost certainly exists and has never been looked for.

1. **Classic:** a FEELING / comprehension / the-act-of-still-reading read as EVIDENCE the reader IS saved. **Reusable models:** S185's *"Any instrument that reads chosen when you feel warm and lost when you feel cold is not measuring the decree. It is measuring the weather. Put it down."*; S186's *"A summons is not verified by the quality of the hearing it produced, and Lazarus was not asked afterward to describe the voice."*; **S188's** — *"The author never asked you to read the decree off your own chest"*; *"The adverb is not a mirror; it is a deed of title, and it was signed in a language you had no vote in"*; *"The door of a tomb does not open from the inside, and Lazarus was not asked afterward to describe how the voice had felt"*; *"Nothing here depends on how the reading felt."*
2. **INVERSE:** absence of a prescribed feeling read as unbelief, or prescribed as the *test of understanding*. **S188's two forms:** `ot-isaiah-servant` universalized a felt undoing as the mark of every saved soul (fixed by making the sequence **God's order of operations, not a memory the reader must produce**); `question-is-salvation-my-choice-or-gods` asserted *"You never wanted to be [the hero]"* — flatly false of many readers. **Never tell a reader what he felt.**
3. **Slips hide in meta / pullquotes / In-Briefs / FAQ-JSON-LD / closing poems / quiz answer keys / BAKED HUB CARDS — and in WIDGET DATA ARRAYS.** **The CLOSING CATCH SECTION is the densest slip real-estate on the site** — 6 of S188's 7 were in the final paragraph.
4. **CONDITIONAL COMFORT.** "*If you can* mean those words, the comfort becomes yours." Own detector class (`--cond`).
