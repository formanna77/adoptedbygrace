Secondary catches the live read keeps surfacing in the CATCH arm — watch for all four:
1. **Doctrinal slips that flatter the reader or make grace the sinner's possession** (S140: invisible-wall-marriage's "you're seeing what only a minority… God chose them to see it" — spiritual-elitism flattery → cut). 2. **Manufactured-scene openers / flickers** that invent the reader's moment (S140: open-wound's "If this page found you at 3 AM — searching for something to hold onto" → cut; invisible-wall's bed-tableau Circular Return → cut). Banned per CLAUDE.md.
3. **Oversold / unverifiable citations** — VERIFY every named study/stat/philosopher/economist/date (S140: Calvin's "four thousand letters / three hundred sermons a year" softened, "four-volume"→"four-book" Institutes; the golden-chain *helkyō* "sword from its sheath" classical usage dropped; secular-economics "45 million"→"as many as 45 million"). On history/theologian/secular/analytical pages this is the #1 risk after the doubled close.
4. **FALSE QUOTATIONS presented as verbatim** (NEW, S140) — an italicized line presented as a direct quote ("one of the most honest sentences in any ancient text") that is actually a modern paraphrase (Augustine's "I was my own prison" → re-rendered faithfully from Confessions 8.5; the golden-chain's fabricated "Luther said it was the most radical trade in the history of the universe" → the Reformers' *great exchange*). When a quote feels suspiciously on-message, it usually is: de-italicize / reframe as paraphrase, or source it.
