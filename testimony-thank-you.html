Testimonies of Grace

Thank You
Your Story Is Received

It is in. A real person will read every word.

Somewhere a real person will read what you wrote, word for word — and if a question comes up before publishing, we will reach you at the email you gave. If your story is added to the wall of the rescued, it will sit alongside Augustine, Newton, and Spurgeon, because the grace that found them is the grace that found you. The same Shepherd. The same unlost sheep.

While you are here, you might rest a while in grace itself, or trace the truth one more turn down with the question beneath every testimony. There is no hurry. The work that saved you was finished before you were born.

You told your story. He authored it. He has you, and He always did.
