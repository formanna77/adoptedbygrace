1. **Doctrinal slips that ground assurance in the reader's FEELING/ache rather than God's prior work** (or make grace the sinner's possession / flatter the reader). (S144 cut Circular Returns on book-of-life, acts7-51, john15, rev3-20, regeneration, fear-control, and softened 1cor15-22's "seminary cousin" opener.) **Distinguish:** a clinical/illustrative time reference (addiction-proof's "wakes 6 AM… by midnight they use" = a day-cycle illustration) is FINE — not a banned crisis scene.
